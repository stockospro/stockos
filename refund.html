↩️ Cancellation & Refund Policy

Last updated: [DATE]

1. Nature of the Product

StockOS Pro licenses are digital goods, delivered instantly upon successful payment. Because the activation key is generated and usable immediately, refund eligibility is limited as described below.

2. Refund Eligibility

  • Failed/duplicate payment: if money was deducted but no key was issued, or you were charged twice, you're entitled to a full refund. Contact us with your payment ID and we will process it within 5–7 business days.
  • Key doesn't work on your device: contact us first — most activation issues (typo, wrong device) can be fixed without a refund by re-issuing the key. If we genuinely cannot resolve it, a full refund will be issued within 5–7 business days.
  • Change of mind: since the license activates instantly on purchase, refunds for change-of-mind after successful activation are generally not available — please use the Test/trial features (if offered) before purchasing.

3. Cancellation

Plans do not auto-renew, so there is nothing to actively "cancel" — simply don't purchase a renewal when your current plan expires.

4. How to Request a Refund

Email or WhatsApp us (see Contact page) with your Razorpay Payment ID and Device ID. We aim to respond within 24 hours and resolve/refund within 5–7 business days.
