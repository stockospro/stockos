This Privacy Policy explains what information StockOS Pro ("we", "us") collects when you purchase or use a license, and how it's used.

1. What We Collect

  • Device ID — a code generated by the app on your device, used only to lock your license to that device.
  • Email address (optional, if provided at checkout) — used to send you a backup copy of your activation key.
  • Payment information — handled entirely by Razorpay. We never see or store your card/UPI/bank details.

2. How We Use It

Solely to generate and deliver your license key, verify your payment, and provide support if you contact us. We do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties for marketing purposes.

3. Data Storage

Device ID, plan, and expiry are stored securely to let you renew or get support later. This data is kept only as long as necessary to provide the Service.

4. Third Parties

We use Razorpay for payment processing and, optionally, Resend for transactional email. Both have their own privacy policies governing the data they process on our behalf.

5. Your Rights

You can request a copy of your data or ask us to delete it by contacting us — see our Contact page.

6. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page.
